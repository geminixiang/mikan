# Karpathy Guidelines

From [Andrej Karpathy's observations](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2015883857489522876):

- Surface assumptions that materially affect the outcome; clarify when needed.
- Prefer the simplest solution that meets the requirement.
- Keep changes scoped and preserve unrelated work.
- Verify the intended behavior with checks suited to the change.

These are judgment aids, not a mandatory sequence or reporting template.
